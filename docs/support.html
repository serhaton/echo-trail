Support

Answers to common questions. If yours is not here, get in touch at the bottom of the page.

I can't hear anything.

Turn the volume up with the side buttons and check Settings › Sound in the game: Effects and Ambience each have their own level. The game plays even when the silent switch is on. If you use Bluetooth headphones, make sure they are connected before you start a level.

Do I need headphones?

They are strongly recommended: the game tells you the direction through your left and right ear. Without headphones the game shows soft wave rings coming from the direction of the sound. You can force either behaviour in Settings › Sound output.

How do I tell whether the sound is in front of me or behind me?

A sound behind you is duller and lower in pitch. The target's strikes also come faster as you get closer, so walk a few steps: if the rhythm speeds up, you are heading the right way.

How do I change the controls?

Three ways to move: follow your finger, a joystick, or tilting the phone. Change them in Settings › Controls, on the card shown before each level, or with the button at the bottom-left during a level.

Tilt controls move me when I'm not tilting.

With tilt controls, “flat” is the resting position: hold the phone level, like a tray. The more you tilt it, the faster you walk.

What do hints do?

A hint shows a light pointing along the way to the sound and briefly reveals the obstacles. You get one free hint per level; more are available by watching a rewarded video.

I bought “Remove ads” but still see ads.

The purchase removes the ads shown between levels. Rewarded videos stay available because you choose to watch them. If ads between levels still appear, open Settings › Purchases and tap “Restore purchases”.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open Settings › Purchases in the game and tap “Restore purchases”.

Is my progress backed up?

Progress is stored on your device. It is included in your device backups, but it is not synced between devices and is deleted if you delete the app.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad, in portrait orientation.
