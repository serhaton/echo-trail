Echo Trail

Find your way by sound

The screen is dark. Somewhere ahead a sound is calling. Follow it with your ears, feel your way past what you cannot see, and watch the place you walked through come to light.

Coming soon to the App StoreFor iPhone and iPad

A game you play with your ears

  • Listen

    Left ear, right ear, louder, clearer, faster: the sound tells you where to go. If it turns dull and low, it is behind you.

  • Feel

    Walls, trees and water are invisible. You hear a soft thud from their side and feel a tap when you touch them.

  • Reveal

    The path you walk glows in colour. Reach the sound and the picture of the place opens and joins your album.

  • Your way

    Follow your finger, use a joystick or tilt the phone. Best with headphones; without them, gentle wave rings show the direction.

Four journeys, 120 levels

  • Zen

    A day-long walk through nature. No rush: when time runs out you simply keep walking.

  • Mystery

    Someone vanished in an old mansion. Thirty rooms, thirty letters, one story.

  • Tension

    Something follows you through the night. Keep moving. Not for young children.

  • Cheerful

    A day at the fair: bright sounds, stars to collect, rainbow trails.
